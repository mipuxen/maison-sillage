Maison Sillage

A concept sneaker boutique on a single page. Sillage (French, n.m.): the trace left behind by something that moves.

Fifteen silhouettes rest on a wall of lit shelves, like a quiet gallery after hours. Choose one and the camera moves in: the shoe leaves its shelf and settles on its own page, where you can turn it over, look at it from every angle, choose your size and put it in your bag.

 **Live demo:** https://mipuxen.github.io/maison-sillage/

Inside the store
The wall. Fifteen pairs on lit shelves. Point at a shoe and its shelf glows and its name appears.
The close-up. Each shoe has its own page with its brand, colour, price and a short story, plus a gallery of up to six angles: side, top, back and sole.
The bag. Pick an EU size, add the pair, change the quantity or remove it, and see the subtotal. The bag stays where you left it, even after you close the page.
Three languages. The whole store speaks English, Uzbek and Korean, and remembers the one you chose.
About and Contact. The idea behind the boutique, who made it, and a place to leave a message.
Any screen. Designed for both desktop and phone, and usable with a keyboard alone.
